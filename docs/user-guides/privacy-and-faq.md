# Privacy, Safety & Frequently Asked Questions

Mihur is designed so **parents manage family accounts** and children practice through private learner links, without a child email or password. Only use approved pilot access instructions and keep sensitive family information out of support messages.

## Who needs an account?

A parent or guardian creates and signs in to the family account, with a separate **four-digit Parent PIN** for sensitive views. Each child has their own nickname-based learner profile and private practice link. Parents can create, replace or revoke links for their linked children.

## What information does Mihur handle?

The public [Privacy Practices](/privacy) page describes the categories handled by Mihur, including parent account information, learner nicknames, curriculum/grade selection and practice evidence. **Learn Together** may store drawings, typed notes or homework images for a board. Avoid uploading school records or identifying details where possible.

Mihur states that AI helps phrase hints while trusted mathematical checks remain in application code. Review the published privacy notice before consent; the Help Center is informational and is not a legal certification of COPPA compliance.

## What if my child's private link stops working?

It may have expired (typically seven days after generation), been revoked, replaced or been used on another device. Ask the parent to open **Parent → Settings & privacy**, unlock with their PIN and **Regenerate link** for *that child*. A regenerated link replaces only that child's old link.

## Can two children share the same link?

**No.** Each child should receive their own practice link and use their own device/browser session. One student's history must not be attributed to a sibling. Parents should avoid using parent sign-in and child practice on the same shared device without signing out and checking which account is active.

## Why can't I open a topic?

Some curricula or mathematical families are still under independent review. On **Explore Topics**, only select skills marked **Ready to practice**. Content that is unpublished or provisional should not be unlocked by selecting a similar-looking skill from another grade or state.

## Can Mihur simply tell my child the answer?

Mihur is designed to build understanding. In eligible guided practice, it may ask helpful questions or show a representation. A mastery check is meant to provide independent evidence, so guided hints must stay separate.

## How do I see progress or delete a learner?

Sign in as the parent and use [Parent dashboard](/parent) for progress or [Settings & privacy](/parent/settings) for parent controls. A deletion is irreversible and differs from revoking a pass. Do not share your PIN.

## Is the Learn Together whiteboard available?

The application contains a family whiteboard with handwriting, text and photo/PDF import routes. **Pilot availability and live multi-device reliability are subject to staging WebSocket, privacy and independent browser QA.** Ask the pilot team whether your family has been approved to use it. Do not treat it as independently assessed mathematics; it is a collaborative workspace.

## Where can I get support?

Use the invitation or pilot communication channel supplied directly by the Mihur team. Include the approximate time, page name, browser type and a description of what happened, but **never send** passwords, PINs, full private links, another student's work or identifying child information.
