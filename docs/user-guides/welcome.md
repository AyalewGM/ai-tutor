# Welcome to Mihur — How It Works

Mihur is a mathematics learning space built to help students understand ideas before memorizing procedures. A parent manages the family account; each child can use a private practice link to work in their own learner space.

**Pilot note:** Some features are implemented but still being independently tested. Your family's available curricula and tools depend on what the pilot team has approved. If a topic says **Content in progress**, it is not yet ready to practice.

## Choose your path

- **Parents:** Start with the [Parent Quick Start](/help/parent-first-day) to create your account, add your child and share a link.
- **Students:** Try the [Student Quick Start](/help/student-first-session) to begin learning independently.
- **Educators:** Read [How Mihur Teaches](/help/progress-and-mastery) to understand the approach. A dedicated educator account or classroom dashboard is not included in this pilot guide.
- **Questions or concerns:** See [Privacy, Safety & FAQs](/help/privacy-and-faq).

## How learning works

Mihur aims to move from **concrete objects → visual models → mathematical symbols**. Depending on the approved skill, a learner may explore a model, work through a problem, receive a guiding question, or complete a fresh independent check.

The teaching sequence is **Observe → Predict → Manipulate → Explain → Practice → Diagnose → Transfer → Verify**. Not every lesson includes every interactive tool, and a practice answer is not automatically proof of mastery.

## Who does what?

**Parent or guardian:** Registers/signs in, creates a four-digit Parent PIN, chooses the family's location, creates one learner profile per child, manages private practice links, and unlocks progress and sensitive settings.

**Student:** Opens their own private link, chooses a ready topic, works through problems, requests help during eligible guided work, and builds independent evidence. Students do not need their own email or password.

**Mihur:** Uses application-owned mathematical checks and approved content to support learning. AI may help phrase selected hints; it is not the trusted grader or decision-maker for mastery.
