# Understanding Progress, Hints & Mastery

Mihur is designed around one idea: a learner should understand *why* a method works, not only recognize the right number.

## Mihur's learning sequence

1. **Observe:** Read the situation and identify the known quantities.
2. **Predict:** Say what should happen before completing the calculation.
3. **Manipulate:** Build or change a picture, number line, fraction bar or other model **when that lesson supports one**.
4. **Explain:** Describe each transformation, including why both sides of an equation stay equal.
5. **Practice:** Try examples with helpful guidance.
6. **Diagnose:** Review the kind of error made, such as a missing negative sign or a skipped operation.
7. **Transfer:** Try a different but related problem rather than repeating a memorized response.
8. **Verify:** Solve independently and check the result using valid mathematical reasoning.

A specific lesson may use only a subset of these steps. Additional interactive models are still under review; an authored lesson description does not mean its visual has been activated.

## Helped success versus independent evidence

During **Guided Practice** or **Remediation**, a learner may receive a question, clue or visual scaffold. That is teaching support. It is not the same as demonstrated mastery.

During **Independent Practice** or a **Mastery Check**, Mihur must use the approved, trusted answer and mode checks. Hints or guided successes do not qualify as independent evidence. A fresh check helps test whether understanding transfers to a new problem.

A progress percentage or badge can encourage practice, but it does not by itself establish readiness for every topic. Parents should focus on recurring strengths and misconceptions, not one score.

## What parents can see

The [parent dashboard](/parent) includes activity and progress views for linked children; detailed views require a Parent PIN. Depending on accepted content and actual practice, it may include attempts, concepts requiring support, reviews due and independently verified mastery.

A low number of practice questions or a topic marked **Content in progress** can indicate that material is not ready in your child's selected curriculum. It does *not* prove that your child has a learning difficulty.

## What AI does — and does not do

Mihur's application-owned mathematical logic determines valid answers, approved learning states and mastery eligibility. Constrained AI services may help phrase explanations or hints. They do **not** independently grade answers or override mastery rules. If a provider is unavailable, built-in guidance can serve eligible lessons.

## A good conversation after practice

Instead of only asking **“How many did you get right?”**, ask:

- “Can you show me why that works?”
- “What changed when you tried another method?”
- “What would you do if the numbers were different?”
