# Parent Quick Start — Your First 10 Minutes

This guide follows the parent registration, practice, settings and PIN controls currently present in Mihur's application. It is written for selected pilot families, not open public enrollment.

## Before you begin

You need an invitation or enrollment instructions from the Mihur pilot team, access to your email account, and the state/province and grade appropriate for each child. Use nicknames or aliases instead of children's full legal names.

**Pilot availability:** Registration may require a single-use invitation code and staff approval. If your account is awaiting approval, do not create another account; contact the pilot team.

## 1. Create your parent account

1. Open the approved Mihur site or your invitation link. Choose **Create account** if the registration form is not already open.
2. Enter your parent email, a strong password, and an optional parent display name.
3. Set your **four-digit Parent PIN**. This is separate from the sign-in password; it unlocks sensitive parent settings and detailed progress views.
4. Select your country and state/province. Read and accept the terms and family privacy notice, and complete the anti-bot challenge if shown.
5. Submit. If you see an approval/waiting state, follow the instructions from the pilot team rather than trying again with a different email.

**Keep your Parent PIN private.** Do not share it with a child. A child's own private link is how they enter practice.

## 2. Add a child

1. As the parent, open **Practice** at [/learn](/learn).
2. In **Add a learner**, enter a short learner nickname, select an avatar and choose an available **exact curriculum** matching the child's grade and school pathway.
3. Select **Add learner**. Repeat for each child your approved pilot entitlement allows.
4. If your region or curriculum isn't available or an option shows **Content in progress**, ask the pilot team which accepted curriculum/skills your family should use. Do not select an unrelated grade merely to unlock more questions.

A child profile keeps learning activity and progress separate from siblings. Seat limits can vary by family pilot entitlement; the pilot does not require you to purchase a subscription.

## 3. Share each child's private practice link

1. Open **Parent → Settings & privacy** at [/parent/settings](/parent/settings).
2. Unlock the page with your four-digit Parent PIN if requested.
3. Under **Private practice links for each child**, find the correct nickname. Select **Generate link**.
4. Select **Copy private link** and send it **privately to that child's device**. The complete link is displayed only immediately after it is created.
5. Repeat separately for every child. Each child must use their *own* link; do not share a sibling's.
6. Links expire after **seven days**. If a link is lost, use **Regenerate link** to replace it, or **Revoke link** to disable it. Regeneration/revocation ends learner sessions created by that child's previous link, not siblings' links.

**Protect the link like a password.** Anyone holding it could attempt to open that child's practice area. Do not put links in public chats, screenshots or school-wide messages. If Mihur says a link is in use on another device, ask the child to finish or contact the parent to regenerate it.

## 4. Check your child's progress

1. Open [Parent dashboard](/parent).
2. Unlock the sensitive progress view using your Parent PIN.
3. Choose the child's profile and review learning activity, concepts needing support, reviews due, and independent progress.

A guided answer, a hint, a badge and a mastery check mean different things. See [Understanding Progress, Hints & Mastery](/help/progress-and-mastery).

## 5. Protect your family account

Review [Settings & privacy](/parent/settings) for privacy notices and parent-managed learner deletion. Deleting a learner's data is different from revoking a link; follow the on-screen confirmation carefully. Sign out of parent accounts on shared devices.

For account problems, see [Privacy, Safety & FAQs](/help/privacy-and-faq). Never send us your password, PIN or full practice-link token.
