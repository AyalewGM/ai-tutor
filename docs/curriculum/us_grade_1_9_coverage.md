# U.S. Grade 1-9 curriculum coverage

Mihur tracks national mathematics expansion as **450 state-grade coverage cells**: 50 states × Grades 1-9.

A cell is complete only when its authoritative source, authority, curriculum version, implemented lifecycle, standards ingestion, reviewed canonical mappings, and publication gates are all satisfied. Proof slices and partial mappings never count as complete.

## Delivery order

Wave 1 prioritizes California, Texas, Florida, New York, Pennsylvania, Illinois, Ohio, Georgia, North Carolina, Michigan, New Jersey, and Virginia.

Existing Maryland and Virginia assets must be reconciled into this matrix and verified against the same completion gate rather than credited automatically.

The matrix is an accounting/control layer only. Learner evidence remains attached to curriculum-local skills and is never copied because two jurisdictions share canonical mathematics.
