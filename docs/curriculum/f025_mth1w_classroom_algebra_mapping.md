# F-025 Ontario MTH1W classroom-aligned algebra mapping

## Authority and scope

Curriculum identity: Ontario Ministry of Education, Grade 9 Mathematics (MTH1W), 2021.
Authoritative source: https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/secondary-mathematics/courses/mth1w

The classroom materials supplied by the owner are sequencing/gap evidence only. They are not product content and must not be copied, transcribed, redistributed, or closely imitated.

## Reviewed expectation boundary

The first F-025 implementation slice is bounded to the official Algebra C1 expectations that support the observed classroom sequence:

- C1.2 — create algebraic expressions that generalize relationships.
- C1.3 — compare algebraic expressions using multiple representations to identify equivalence.
- C1.4 — simplify algebraic expressions using properties of operations and multiple representations.
- C1.5 — create and solve equations in context and verify solutions.

This slice deepens C1.3/C1.4 with curriculum-local skills for like-term structure and polynomial addition/subtraction. Existing expression/equation skills remain in place and are mapped to the more specific C1 expectations.

## Pedagogy and prerequisite decision

The application owns the decomposition and graph. For this bounded sequence:

`MTH1W.C.ALG.EXPR -> MTH1W.C.ALG.LIKE -> MTH1W.C.ALG.POLY`

The graph represents reviewed mathematical dependencies for this product slice, not teacher worksheet order. Equation skills retain their existing path from the expression skill and are not made dependent on polynomial addition/subtraction merely because those topics may appear nearby in a classroom unit.

## Original content and variation

New problems are AI Tutor-authored. Parameterized generators provide fresh like-term and polynomial addition/subtraction variants with deterministic canonical answers. No worksheet question, diagram, or wording is stored in the repository.

## Data impact and isolation

Tests use synthetic/minimized data. Ontario mastery/evidence remains keyed to Ontario curriculum-local skill identities and is not transferred to Maryland or other curricula.
