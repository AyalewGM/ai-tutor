## Findings

1. **Every unmapped expectation is PS or PFA.** The `va-gradeN-sol-2024_25`
   packs contain no Probability & Statistics and no Patterns, Functions &
   Algebra skills at any grade. The entire data-handling and early-algebra
   thread (13 expectations, 20% of the framework) has zero repository
   candidates — the largest coherent elementary gap.

2. **All 51 "provisional" links are strand-level, not expectation-level.**
   Pack skills carry `standard_refs` like `"3.CE"` rather than `"3.CE.1"`.
   Links in this manifest were generated by strand-prefix match and are
   `REPOSITORY_EXISTING_UNVERIFIED` — they indicate a candidate skill exists
   in the right strand, nothing more. None may be reported as coverage.

3. **Pack skills are composites by construction.** e.g. `VA3.NF.FRACTIONS`
   plausibly touches 3.NS.3(a–d) and 3.CE.* fraction work simultaneously.
   Composite skills cannot transfer mastery per the #293 review precedent;
   decomposition proposals belong in the Standards Mapping lane.

4. **Seed/pack taxonomy predates the 2023 strand scheme.** Pack codes use
   legacy-style strands (`VA3.OA.*`, `VA4.NBT.*`, `VA5.NF.*`) while the 2023
   SOL uses NS/CE/MG/PS/PFA. Any accepted mapping must reconcile these naming
   generations explicitly — they are not equivalent partitions.
