## Six independent readiness dimensions

Every expectation is classified separately for:

1. repository standards mapping,
2. independently reviewed mathematical skill,
3. exact-target generator availability,
4. independently validated practice,
5. assessment readiness,
6. verified mastery evidence.

A repository row, starter problem, test, or generator cannot establish the later dimensions. All 30 expectations remain fail-closed for accepted coverage and learner mastery.

## Reconciliation findings

1. The traditional pack's 15 local skills are useful content inventory, but their generated `MATH.ALGEBRA1.*` targets are not expectation-level MSDE mappings.
2. The Integrated Algebra I seed currently marks all 13 rows `EQUIVALENT`; that claim needs independent decomposition review because several official expectations contain multiple observable competencies.
3. `IA1.AT.A.1`, `IA1.AT.C.10`, `IA1.AT.D.14`, and `IA1.DS.B.6` are especially composite and should not receive full coverage credit from one procedural item.
4. PR #308's six reviewed foundational candidates do not directly authorize any Grade 9 IA1 mapping. Production taxonomy remains empty.
5. Historical course rows and learner evidence must remain untouched. Any later mapping layer must be additive and versioned.
